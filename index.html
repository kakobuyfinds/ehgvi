From discovery to decision

How to use a Sugargoo spreadsheet

A Sugargoo spreadsheet is a research list that helps organize products, source links, prices and purchase notes. Use the directory to discover options, then confirm each item with your chosen shopping service before paying.

01 / DISCOVER

Find an exact product

Filter the catalog by category and item price. Open CAFANS and search for the complete product name. Save the original listing URL and the color, size or model you want; a general product name may describe several options.

02 / VERIFY

Check purchase eligibility

Confirm that your shopping agent accepts the exact listing and can serve your destination. A product appearing in a directory does not establish current stock, authenticity, permitted purchase or shipping eligibility.

03 / COMPARE

Review the complete cost

Record the item price separately from domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable destination charges. Compare the same product option and quantity across quotes.

Better comparison notes

What to check before you commit

Sizing and measurements

  • Record the size chart for the exact listing.
  • Compare clothing measurements with a garment you own.
  • Keep shoe sizing systems and measurement units visible.
  • Ask about missing measurements before ordering.

QC photos and condition

  • Distinguish seller images from photos of your actual item.
  • Review the selected color, quantity and visible condition.
  • Request useful close-ups where details are unclear.
  • Photographs alone cannot establish authenticity or durability.

Shipping and returns

  • Request a quote for your destination and parcel.
  • Check how parcel dimensions affect the quote.
  • Confirm return deadlines and who pays return delivery.
  • Record unresolved charges before approving payment.

Build a shortlist you can revisit

Keep one row per exact product option. Record the product name, source URL, seller, category, selected size, color, quantity, original currency and the date you checked the price. Add separate columns for measurements, shipping estimates and unanswered questions.

Use clear statuses such as “researching,” “confirmed” and “unavailable.” Before purchasing, reopen the original listing and update your notes. A saved price or screenshot is a record of an earlier check rather than a guarantee of the current offer.

Sugargoo spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official Sugargoo website?

No. This is an independent product research guide. Its catalog excerpt comes from CAFANS, and the shopping buttons lead to CAFANS.

Can every product here be ordered through Sugargoo?

Compatibility has not been verified. Confirm the exact source listing, selected option and destination with Sugargoo before placing an order.

Are these prices live and inclusive of shipping?

No. They are estimated USD item prices from a CAFANS homepage snapshot recorded November 19, 2026. Confirm the latest item price and all additional charges at the source.

Why do the buttons take me to CAFANS?

CAFANS is the source directory for this page. Open the catalog there and search for the complete product name to continue researching the listing.

Does this page include verified QC images?

No. This excerpt lists product names, categories and estimated prices. Request photos of the actual item through your chosen service when available; seller photographs should not be presented as independent quality checks.

How should I compare two similar finds?

Compare the exact size, color, quantity, measurements and stated specifications. Then record the total quoted cost and current return terms. Leave missing information marked as unknown until confirmed.
